# Music Files

Place your ambient background music file here as `ambient.mp3`.

## Recommendations

- **Format**: MP3 (best browser support)
- **Duration**: 2-5 minutes (will loop)
- **Style**: Ambient, Japanese koto, nature sounds, or peaceful instrumental
- **Volume**: Keep it subtle (component sets volume to 30%)

## Free Music Resources

You can find royalty-free ambient music at:
- [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/)
- [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/)
- [YouTube Audio Library](https://www.youtube.com/audiolibrary)

## Note

The music player is muted by default. Users can enable it by clicking the music button in the top-right corner.
